# Research Standard

## Objective
Gather only enough reliable evidence to support the decision.

## Source Priority
Prefer:
1. official documentation/API/specification/source
2. vendor technical documentation
3. reputable independent technical sources
4. community evidence for practical issues

Do not use marketing claims as sole evidence for material technical capabilities.

## Evidence Labels
- VERIFIED: authoritative direct evidence
- CORROBORATED: multiple credible sources
- LIKELY: strong inference, not directly verified
- UNCERTAIN: incomplete evidence
- CONFLICTING: credible sources disagree
- UNKNOWN: not established
- ASSUMPTION: unverified working belief
- RECOMMENDATION: proposed action based on evidence

## What Requires Verification
Verify only when material to the decision, especially:
- required API/capability
- authentication/access
- restrictions/rate limits
- pricing/licensing
- compatibility/integration
- security/data boundaries

Check freshness for changing products, pricing, APIs, AI models and integrations.

## Research Efficiency
- Start with decision-changing questions.
- Search broadly enough to find credible alternatives.
- Investigate only viable candidates deeply.
- Stop investigating a branch once a hard constraint eliminates it.
- Do not duplicate already verified facts unless disputed or stale.
- If the answer cannot be established efficiently, mark UNKNOWN and state the validation required.

## Traceability
Material conclusions must identify the supporting source.
