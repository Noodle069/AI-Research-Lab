Date: 2026-09-25. Author: main agent. Queensland (Gold Coast) only.
Built from: Luca's resume (documents/), research/02, reviews/01, and two new searches (sources at the end).

## Bottom line

Luca already has the things that get most applicants screened out: Cert II, White Card, P plates and own car, work experience, a job. His chances now depend less on getting more qualifications and more on:
1. **Using his existing contacts** (the electrician, RTD, Trade College) to get introductions.
2. **Getting in front of the right Gold Coast employers before 1 Jan 2027**, while their incentive is higher.
3. **Passing the aptitude test.**
4. **Being ready to start on day one.** That means LVR and CPR done and a start date set.
